# Curated sources for roadmaps

These are the lists the owner starred. Each one is free, community maintained and large, so pick the right section instead of linking the whole repository.

## Computer science as a degree

- **OSSU computer-science** (github.com/ossu/computer-science). A complete, free undergraduate path: intro programming, core CS (programming, maths, tools, systems, theory, security, applications), then advanced electives and a final project. Use its stage order as the backbone for any "learn computer science" request. Link the README sections for each stage.

## Job-role roadmaps

- **developer-roadmap** (github.com/nilbuild/developer-roadmap, the roadmap.sh project). Step-by-step charts for roles such as frontend, backend, DevOps, AI engineer, data analyst and cybersecurity. Use it when the owner names a job rather than a subject. Link the role's chart on roadmap.sh.

## Free books and courses

- **free-programming-books** (github.com/EbookFoundation/free-programming-books). Free books, courses, podcasts and interactive tutorials, sorted by language and by subject, in many human languages. Use its "books by subject" and "courses" lists to fill each stage.
- **papers-we-love** (github.com/papers-we-love/papers-we-love). Classic computer science papers sorted by topic (distributed systems, databases, machine learning and more). Add one paper to an advanced stage, not to a beginner one.

## System design and engineering practice

- **system-design-primer** (github.com/donnemartin/system-design-primer). How large systems are built: scaling, caching, databases, queues, with interview questions and worked designs. Use for backend, architecture and interview stages.
- **engineering-blogs** (github.com/kilimchoi/engineering-blogs). Engineering blogs of companies and people. Suggest two or three to follow once the owner is past the basics.

## Free tools and APIs to build with

- **free-for-dev** (github.com/ripienaar/free-for-dev). Services with free tiers (hosting, databases, CI, email). Use it when a project needs somewhere to deploy.
- **public-apis** (github.com/public-apis/public-apis). Free public APIs by category. Good for small practice projects that need real data.
- **open-source-alternatives** (github.com/btw-so/open-source-alternatives). Open-source replacements for paid tools.
- **awesome-free-llm-apis** (github.com/mnfst/awesome-free-llm-apis). Free LLM API providers, already used by Prometheus's model catalog.
